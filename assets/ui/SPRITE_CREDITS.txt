Gen 3 ball and item sprites
==========================

Source: PokéSprite (https://github.com/msikma/pokesprite)
Files: 32x32 PNG inventory sprites.

The ball icons live in assets/ui/balls/ and use the Gen 3 ball slugs from
src/core_data.py.  The held-item icons live in assets/ui/items/ and use the
same item slugs; TM01-TM50 share the single tm-normal.png icon, and
unknown.png is the fallback for an item id without a sprite.  A held Poké Ball
is an item, but its badge resolves to assets/ui/balls/<slug>.png first, so a
held Master Ball/Great Ball/etc. never falls back to the unknown-item icon.
